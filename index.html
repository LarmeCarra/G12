Sir. Garry

We sincerely express our heartfelt gratitude for your dedication in teaching Physics, Biology, and Earth and Life Science. Your passion and commitment have made every lesson meaningful and engaging. We are especially thankful for the memorable activities and assignments you prepared, which not only strengthened our understanding but also made learning enjoyable and unforgettable. Your incredible performance as an educator has truly inspired us to appreciate the wonders of science and to strive for excellence.






Papa Eds

We extend our deepest appreciation for your guidance and dedication in teaching Chemistry and Research. Your expertise and enthusiasm have greatly influenced our learning journey. Your remarkable performance and unwavering support have motivated us to push beyond our limits and pursue knowledge with confidence and curiosity.






Sir Dino
